Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need experience to join a workshop?

No — every workshop is written for first-timers. Instructors assume zero prior experience and walk you through each tool before you touch it.

How fast do online orders ship?

Orders over ₹999 dispatch within the same week, every time. You'll get a tracking link by email as soon as it leaves the shop.

Can I return supplies if they're the wrong fit?

Unopened items can be returned within 14 days of purchase. Bring your receipt or order confirmation to the shop, or contact us to arrange a courier pickup.

Do you offer classes for kids?

Yes — we run beginner-friendly sessions for kids aged 8 and up, covering drawing, clay, and simple crafting projects.

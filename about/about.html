Our story

Built for makers, stocked with care.

ArtCraft Supply Studio started as a single shelf of watercolors and a lot of stubbornness. Today it's a full shop on Road No. 12 — but the goal hasn't changed: give every maker in Hyderabad the supplies and the confidence to start.

Our mission

Make starting a craft less intimidating.

Every product we stock and every workshop we run comes back to one question: does this help someone pick up a brush, some clay, or a needle for the first time? If the answer is no, we don't carry it.

  • Staff who'll happily explain the difference between gouache and acrylic
  • Fair pricing on beginner kits, not just professional-grade gear
  • Weekly workshops designed for people who've never tried the medium
Our journey

From one shelf to a full studio.

A short version of how ArtCraft grew from a single watercolor shelf into a shop, a workshop space, and a small community.

2019

One shelf, one shopkeeper

ArtCraft opened as a single aisle of watercolors and sketchbooks inside a stationery shop on Road No. 12.

2021

A shop of our own

We moved into our current space and expanded into clay, fabric, and mixed-media supplies.

2023

First workshop calendar

What started as one Saturday class became a weekly schedule across painting, pottery, and stitching.

2026

1,200+ products, 150+ workshops

Today ArtCraft stocks every medium under one roof and hosts makers of every skill level, every week.
